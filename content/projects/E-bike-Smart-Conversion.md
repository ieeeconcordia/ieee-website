This is our electrical-focused build: we’re converting a normal bicycle into a 48 V smart e-bike across two terms (Winter + Summer), with:

* Governed “road mode” (limited assist to match local e-bike rules)
* Off-street lab mode for controlled testing on campus/private tracks
* Regenerative braking support (where the drivetrain/controller supports it)
* Road-legal lighting + horn + safety systems (brake-cut levers, torque arms, fusing)
* A custom PCB harness + energy analytics (voltage/current logging, Wh/km, range stats, regen event capture)

Why (this is THE electrical project people asked for):If you want something that feels like “real electrical engineering,” this hits a ton of it in one platform:

* Power & energy: 48 V battery pack, BMS, fusing, charging, power budgeting
* Circuits & integration: wiring diagrams, connectors, protection, grounding/returns, noise issues
* Embedded systems: microcontroller logging, sensors, data acquisition, storage
* Measurement: voltage/current sensing, calibration, accuracy validation
* Systems & safety: fault modes, thermal considerations, strain relief, reliability testing
* Compliance & human factors: road-mode limiting, lighting/horn, operational checklists
