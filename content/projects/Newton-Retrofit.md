We’re upgrading our existing quadruped robot Newton into a higher-performance, autonomy-ready platform over two terms (Winter + Summer). The build focuses on:

* High-torque actuation + lower leg inertia (more torque, smoother motion, better efficiency)
* A stronger drivetrain redesign — we’re currently deciding between two transmission approaches, with capstan drives as one option, second being a QDD
* Autonomy stack upgrades: adding a 3D LiDAR and bringing up SLAM (mapping + localization) for reliable indoor/outdoor navigation
