Curious about how real engineering teams stay organized and deliver results efficiently? This workshop will introduce you to the essentials of Agile and Scrum, two of the most widely used project management frameworks in the engineering world.

Through fun, hands-on activities, you’ll see how these methods improve teamwork, communication, and project flow.
We’ll also explore Notion, a powerful digital workspace that goes far beyond simple note-taking. You’ll learn how to use it to plan projects, track tasks, and collaborate like a pro, all in one place.

Bring your laptop with Notion set up and logged in. No experience needed, just curiosity and a team spirit!
