Join us for Robowars, where teams design and build fully autonomous sumo-bots to compete head-to-head in an electrifying robotics showdown! Your robot's mission: detect, engage, and push your opponent out of the ring - all on its own.

🚨 IMPORTANT: You MUST have a team of 1-6 people to compete. We will NOT form teams for you. Use our Discord server to find teammates before registering.

What You'll Do:

* For the next few months, design and build your own autonomous Arduino-based sumo-bot with your team
* Compete in a traditional Japanese Dohyo (sumo ring)
* Battle against teams from across Quebec
* Network with industry professionals and fellow robotics enthusiasts

Competition Details:

* Robot Requirements: Fully autonomous, 20cm x 20cm, max 3kg
* Format: Best 2 out of 3 matches, 3-minute battles
* Equipment: Starter module required (details provided after registration)
* Safety: All participants must wear provided safety equipment

Team Requirements:

* Team Size: 1-6 students per team (REQUIRED)
* You must form your own team - organizers will not match participants
* Join our Discord: Find teammates, ask questions, and connect with other teams
* All team members must register individually

Event Details:

* When: May 16, 2026
* Where: Concordia University, Montreal
* Open to: Anyone

How to Register:

1. Find or form a team of 1-6 people
2. Join our Discord server: [https://discord.gg/f6MbwjyHC6](https://discord.gg/f6MbwjyHC6)
3. Each team member registers individually below
4. Indicate your team name and teammates when registering
