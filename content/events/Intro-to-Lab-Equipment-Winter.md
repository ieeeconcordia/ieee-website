Want to get hands-on with electronics?
Join IEEE Concordia for a practical workshop where you’ll learn the basics of circuits and lab equipment! Build simple circuits on breadboards, discover key components like resistors and diodes, and learn how to use multimeters, oscilloscopes, and power supplies (AC & DC).

Perfect for students taking ELEC 273, ELEC 275, or anyone curious about electronics. No prior experience needed!
