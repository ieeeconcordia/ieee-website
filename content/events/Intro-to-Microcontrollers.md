Want to get started with microcontrollers and DIY electronics?

Join us for a hands-on ESP32 workshop where you’ll learn how to program a microcontroller and build simple interactive projects using sensors and basic electronic components.

🎁 Your ticket includes a full ESP32 starter kit (ESP32 microcontroller, sensors, breadboard, wires, and more) that you’ll use during the workshop and keep after the event to continue building your own projects.

Perfect for beginners, students, and anyone curious about embedded systems. No prior experience needed.

📌 Make sure to bring your laptop with the Arduino IDE installed.
