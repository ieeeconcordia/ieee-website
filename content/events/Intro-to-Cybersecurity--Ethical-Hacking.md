Curious about how hackers actually think and operate?

Join us for a beginner-friendly, hands-on workshop focused on web application hacking and real-world cybersecurity practices.

You’ll explore how attackers test systems, how vulnerabilities are discovered, and how cybersecurity professionals protect applications. The session is designed to be very practical, with guided exercises to help you get your first experience in ethical hacking.

We’ll be working in a Linux (Kali) environment, using industry-relevant tools and platforms to simulate real attack scenarios.

Perfect for beginners, students, and anyone curious about cybersecurity. No prior experience needed.
