## About the Event

Want to turn your circuit ideas into real hardware? 

Join us for a hands-on Printed Circuit Board (PCB) Design workshop where you’ll learn how to go from schematic to a complete PCB using KiCad. You’ll implement your circuit schematic, understand how components connect, and move into PCB layout and routing all in one session. By the end of the workshop, you’ll have a solid foundation in PCB design and a project ready for manufacturing. 

Perfect for Electrical Engineering students, electronics enthusiasts, and anyone interested in prototyping and product design. No prior experience required (just make sure to bring your laptop with KiCad installed).
