Want to manage your projects better and collaborate without losing your work?

Join us for this beginner friendly Intro to Git workshop and learn how to track changes, organize your files, and work efficiently with others.

You’ll learn the basics of Git, including commits, branching, pushing to GitHub, and handling simple merge conflicts. By the end of the session, you’ll know how to keep your projects clean and well organized.
