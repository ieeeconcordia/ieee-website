## About the Event

🛠️ Ever wanted to design and 3D print your own creations?

Join our Intro to 3D Printing workshop and learn how to model in Fusion 360, slice in Bambu Studio, and bring your ideas to life!

🤝 In Collaboration With:

Pixel au Produit,  a local 3D printing company for students, proudly owned and operated by students!

🌐 Visit them at: [https://pixelauproduit.ca/en/](https://pixelauproduit.ca/en/ "https://pixelauproduit.ca/en/")

## What You Need

💻 Your laptop with Fusion 360 + Bambu Studio installed (details sent with your ticket) + your creativity!
